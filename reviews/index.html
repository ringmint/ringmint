How Ring Mint collects reviews

Every client is invited to review on Trustpilot after their piece is delivered. Reviews are published there first, by the client, and only then reproduced here. Ring Mint does not edit them beyond removing typographical dashes for house style, does not pay for them, and does not remove critical ones. If a review ever raises a problem, the reply and the fix will be public on Trustpilot too.

Is Ring Mint legitimate?

Yes. Ring Mint is the concierge business of Chloe Alpert, who has worked in the jewelry trade since 2009 and grew up in her mother's business, Teaman Jewelry, founded in 1979 in Pennsylvania. Chloe holds the Cert GA from Gem-A, the Gemmological Association of Great Britain. Every ring ships with an independent appraisal and the stone's IGI or GIA grading report, deposits and refunds are set out in writing on the returns and warranty page, and all shipping is fully insured. The full founder background is on the about page; press coverage and quotable facts are on the press page.
