A 3ct marquise engagement ring upgrade, with the whole family tucked inside

By Chloe Alpert · September 12, 2026 · 3 min read

I want to show you the inside of this ring.

The center diamond is a 3ct marquise, set in 18k yellow gold. We’ll get to that. But tucked inside the band are four little stones: one for the husband, one for the wife, and one for each of their two kids.

That’s the detail I love about this engagement ring upgrade. You look at it from above and see a beautiful diamond. Turn it over, and their whole family is there.

The ring, from the front

Here’s the view everyone else gets.

The marquise gives this ring its long, pointed shape. There’s plenty happening already with a center stone like this, so the family detail sits inside the band, where it feels like something just for the person wearing it.

An upgrade with a little more of their story in it

By the time someone comes to me for an engagement ring upgrade, there can be a lot of life behind that original ring.

For this family, there are now four people to represent. We put that into the new ring in a way you might miss entirely unless someone showed you.

I like that you don’t have to explain it to everyone. You can just know it’s there.

This is also why I love working on custom engagement rings. We get to talk about details like this. Something small can become the part of the ring that means the most.

Here it is on a hand

I always want to see this view, too. The close-ups show the setting, but an on-hand photograph helps you picture the whole ring.

If you’re thinking about an upgrade, send me pictures of what you wear now. Tell me what you still love about it and what you’d change. If there’s a wedding band you want to keep wearing, I want to see that, too.

You don’t need to arrive knowing the name of every setting. “I love this part, but this bit bothers me” is useful information.

Then this message came through

I sent a photograph showing the four stones inside the band. This was the response:

“That is absolutely insane. Its beautiful. Exactly what I was picturing”

My response was: “We nailed it 🙌🙌 teamwork makes the dream work and this was meant to be.”

Which pretty much sums it up.

“Exactly what I was picturing” is such a good message to get. We’ve been talking about an idea, working through the details, and then there it is: a real ring with the little things they wanted in it.

And yes, we talked about the budget

We completed this ring at 40% below retail, based on our comparison for this project.

That’s specific to this commission. Your quote will depend on your diamond, metal, and setting, so I’d rather talk through what you want than promise the same percentage on every ring.

But the budget is absolutely part of the conversation. Tell me what you’re comfortable spending. We can work through the choices with that in mind.
