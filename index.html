Meet your concierge

I was at my first jewelry show when I was five days old.

Hi, I’m Chloe.

My mom started Teaman Jewelry in 1974. I grew up in her studio watching her make things by hand, and on my hands and knees under the bench, chasing down diamonds that had rolled away. Later I went to London and earned my gemology certification from the Gem-A.

Ring Mint is how I keep doing what I love: sitting with someone, working out what they actually want, and finding the right stone for it at a price no retail case can touch. Engagement rings, wedding bands, a necklace for an anniversary, a pair of earrings, resetting a stone that belonged to your grandmother. If it can be made, we make it.

Stones too. Lab and natural diamonds, sapphires, emeralds, spinels, and the ultra-rare things you can only get if you know the right people. That is what 50+ years of relationships buys you, and it is the part of this business you cannot start from scratch.

This has been my family’s work since 1974, and it has been a part of me since day one. When you email or text Ring Mint, you get me and our family’s 50+ year history.

About Ring Mint

A modern concierge backed by decades of jewelry experience.

Ring Mint is backed by Teaman Jewelry, our family business, which has been making fine jewelry since 1974.

The model is intentionally different: no showroom, no inventory, and no reason to steer you toward a stone or setting that is not right for you. We source globally, design from scratch, and make each piece only after it is approved.

We work across the full range: engagement rings, bands, necklaces, earrings, bracelets, and heirloom redesigns, in lab and natural diamonds, colored gemstones, and rare high-end stones. Five decades of trade relationships are what make the hard-to-find ones findable.

The result is a more personal process, better pricing, and a piece that feels like it could only belong to one person.
