About Ring Mint

Most jewelers buy diamonds and finished rings before they know who wants them, then carry the cost of a showroom and sales staff. Those costs are recovered in the price of whatever sells, and the inventory already in the case creates a quiet pressure to steer clients toward it.

Ring Mint inverts that. A client describes what they want, and only then is the stone sourced against that brief from a network of more than two million natural and lab created diamonds. The ring is designed from scratch in CAD, refined with the client, and manufactured to order in about two weeks. There is no showroom, no held inventory, and no separate concierge fee.

The company was founded in 2025 by Chloe Alpert, a second-generation jeweler, with the support of her family's business, Teaman Jewelry, in operation since 1974. Ring Mint works with clients across the United States and internationally.

Quotable statements

Attributable to Chloe Alpert, founder of Ring Mint. Free to quote without checking back.

On the inventory model

“Most jewelers buy the stone before they know who wants it. Then they have to sell you that stone. I source after you tell me what you want, so I have no reason to steer you anywhere.”

On overpaying

“Someone shows me their ring and tells me what they paid, and I can see exactly where the money went, and it wasn't into the ring.”

On lab created diamonds

“Lab diamonds are chemically and physically identical to mined ones. But their resale value is poor and falling, and natural diamonds resell well below retail too. Neither one is an investment. Buy the ring you want to look at, not the one you think you'll sell.”

On the family business

“I've always been Sharon's daughter in this industry, never Chloe. It's a title I'm proud to wear.”

On the wax model

“People agonize over carat weight on a screen. I'd rather mail you a wax model so you can see it on the actual hand. It's talked more than one client into a smaller stone, and a happier one.”
