Custom engagement rings

Engagement rings designed from scratch, not picked from a case.

A custom engagement ring is modeled digitally to your exact specifications and manufactured only after you approve it. Nothing is pulled from inventory, which means no design compromises and no markup on a stone that has been sitting in a safe.

In short

Ring Mint designs custom engagement rings in CAD, sources natural or lab created diamonds from a global network of over two million stones, sends a wax model so you can try the shape on your hand, and manufactures the finished ring in about two weeks. There is no showroom and no inventory.

What “custom” actually means

Most large retailers use “custom” to describe a configurator: you choose one of a few dozen preset settings, pick a metal, and drop in a stone. The setting itself already exists. That is customization, and it is genuinely useful, but it is not the same thing.

A fully custom ring starts from a blank file. The band width, the prong count and shape, the height of the basket, the gallery detail under the stone, the taper of the shank, the exact millimeter dimensions of the center stone and every accent — all of it is a decision rather than a default. If you want a cathedral setting that sits 1mm lower because your partner works with their hands, that is a change you can make. In a configurator it is not an option that exists.

The practical difference shows up in two places: rings that have to fit an existing wedding band, and rings built around an unusual stone shape or an heirloom stone. Both are routine in custom work and close to impossible off a shelf.

Why designing from scratch usually costs less, not more

The intuition is that bespoke should be the expensive option. In jewelry it often is not, and the reason is structural rather than clever.

A store with a display case has to buy stones and finished rings before it knows who will want them. That capital sits in a safe, sometimes for years. The business also pays rent on retail space, staffs a sales floor, and absorbs the cost of everything that never sells. All of it has to be recovered somewhere, and the only place it can be recovered is the price of the pieces that do sell.

Ring Mint holds no inventory and has no storefront. Stones are sourced after you have described what you want, and the ring is manufactured after you have approved the design. The cost of guessing wrong — the single biggest hidden expense in traditional retail — is not in the price, because nobody is guessing.

The honest caveat: the center stone is usually most of the total, and a stone costs roughly what a stone costs. The savings come from the setting, the sourcing margin, and the absence of retail overhead — not from magic.

How the design process works

Seven steps, start to finish. The full breakdown lives on the home page timeline, and the short version is this:

  1. You get in touch with whatever you have — a Pinterest board, a photo, a rough budget, or nothing but a deadline.
  2. You meet your concierge on a call and ask everything. There is no script and no sales floor.
  3. Together you set the budget, the proposal date, and the stone priorities — size, shape, and whether certification or spread matters more to you.
  4. We design the ring in CAD and search a global network of more than two million stones against your brief.
  5. You review renderings, request changes, and can have a wax model sent to try on at home.
  6. You approve the final design and the ring is manufactured to order in about two weeks.
  7. It ships fully insured with an independent appraisal and final photographs.

The part clients consistently say they did not expect: how much of it happens over text. Most of the back-and-forth is messages and shared images, not scheduled appointments.

Common questions

How much does a custom engagement ring cost?

A custom engagement ring generally costs the same or less than a comparable ring bought from a retail case, because the setting is made to order and the stone is sourced for you rather than marked up as held inventory.

The stone drives most of the price. We work to a budget you set at the start and quote the stone and the setting separately, so you can see exactly where the money is going and move it between the two.

How long does a custom engagement ring take to make?

Production takes about two weeks once the design is approved. The design phase before that depends entirely on how quickly decisions get made — some clients approve a CAD in a few days, others spend several weeks refining.

If you have a proposal date, say so at the start and we build the timeline backwards from it.

Can I see the ring before it is made?

Yes. You review a CAD rendering of the exact ring before anything is manufactured, and you can request a wax model to try on at home so you can judge the scale on the hand. Nothing goes into production until you approve it.

What is the difference between custom and customizable?

Customizable means choosing from a retailer's preset options — a fixed setting in one of three metals with a stone dropped in. Custom means the ring is modeled from scratch to your proportions, so the band width, prong style, gallery, basket height, and stone dimensions are all decisions rather than defaults.

Can you match a ring I found online or on Pinterest?

Yes, and it is one of the most common places a design starts. Send the images.

We can reproduce the look, adjust the proportions to your hand and budget, or use it as a starting point and change the parts you are unsure about.
