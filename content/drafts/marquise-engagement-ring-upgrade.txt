<h2>An upgrade with a little more of their story in it</h2>
<p>By the time someone comes to me for an engagement ring upgrade, there can be a lot of life behind that original ring.</p>
<p>For this family, there are now four people to represent. We put that into the new ring in a way you might miss entirely unless someone showed you.</p>
<p>I like that you don’t have to explain it to everyone. You can just know it’s there.</p>
<p>This is also why I love working on <a href="/custom-engagement-rings/" class="text-link">custom engagement rings</a>. We get to talk about details like this. Something small can become the part of the ring that means the most.</p>
