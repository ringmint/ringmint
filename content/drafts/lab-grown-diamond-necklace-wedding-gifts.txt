<p>A client needed three diamond necklaces as wedding gifts for the mothers and a sister. They had been seeing retail options between $2,000 and $3,500 per necklace.</p>
<p>We started with CAD, sourced the diamonds, and finished all three necklaces in our Pennsylvania studio. Each features an approximately 6mm round lab-grown diamond, D or E color, VVS2 clarity, and an individual IGI grading report.</p>
<p>Two necklaces are in 14k yellow gold. <p>The brief was a set of gifts for three people close to the wedding. A diamond solitaire necklace gives each recipient the same simple design, while the choice of yellow or white gold makes room for individual preferences.</p>
<p>We kept the focus on a single round diamond in a four-prong setting. Two pieces use the warmth of 14k yellow gold; the third pairs the diamond with 14k white gold.</p>

<h2>Then, sourcing the lab-grown diamonds</h2>
<p>For this commission, we sourced three approximately 6mm round lab-grown diamonds. Their color grades are D or E, and all three have VVS2 clarity and individual IGI reports.</p>
<p>Those specifications describe the stones selected for this order. A photograph can show the appearance of a necklace, but it cannot establish a diamond’s color grade, clarity, or identity.</p>
<p>We describe the size in millimeters because that was part of the brief. Approximately 6mm refers to the diamond’s diameter; it is not a carat weight. Each stone’s exact weight and measurements belong on its individual report.</p>
